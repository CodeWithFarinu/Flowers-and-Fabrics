Frequently Addressed Inquiries

What is the minimum yardage cut for luxury fabrics?

Our master cutters enforce a minimum of 4 yards for Senator fabrics and 5 yards for Swiss Lace to preserve pattern continuity. We cut in 0.5-yard increments.

How does Lagos Same-Day Dispatch work?

Orders placed before 1:00 PM West Africa Time (WAT) with Lagos delivery addresses are handed directly to our dedicated motorcycle dispatch riders for delivery between 3:30 PM – 5:30 PM.

Can I request a verified Master Tailor referral?

Yes. Upon checkout or via our WhatsApp Concierge, patrons can select complimentary referral to accredited Master Tailors located in Victoria Island, Ikoyi, Lekki Phase 1, and Maitama Abuja.

What is the "Get a worth for ur class" pledge?

Our founding promise: Every bolt is authenticated under Certificate #ATL-NG-88921-2026, inspected for yarn tension and tropical durability, and backed by a 14-day exchange privilege.
